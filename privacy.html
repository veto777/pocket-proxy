Pocket Proxy privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026 (updated for Cellular Shield). Pocket Proxy is published by Construct Hub LLC.

Summary

Pocket Proxy collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us.

Cellular Shield (VPN)

Cellular Shield is a VPN that runs entirely on your iPhone. It sends your iPhone's internet traffic over your cellular data connection instead of the Wi-Fi network you are joined to. There is no Pocket Proxy server: the traffic goes from your iPhone straight to the websites and services you use, carried by your mobile carrier. The Shield does not read, store, log or share the content of your traffic, the websites you visit or your DNS lookups; it only counts bytes and connections in memory on your iPhone to show you how much data went through it.

We do not sell, use or disclose to third parties any data from the Shield or the proxy, for any purpose. We have no access to it.

Traffic through the proxy

The proxy passes traffic between your own computer and the websites and services it connects to. The app does not read, store, change or share that traffic. To show you what is happening, the app keeps a short list of recent connections (destination address and byte counts) in memory on your iPhone only; it is erased when the app closes and never leaves the device.

Your public IP address

To show your public IP address, the app makes a request to api.ipify.org, a free third-party service, when the proxy starts and when you tap Check or Test the proxy (the test request travels through the proxy over plain HTTP). Like any website, that service sees your iPhone's public IP address. No other information is sent.

Data on your iPhone

Your settings (port, username, options) are stored on your iPhone. The proxy password is stored in the iPhone keychain and is never sent anywhere except when your own computer presents it to the proxy.

Local network

If you turn on Allow Wi-Fi connections, iPhone asks for permission to use the local network so computers on your Wi-Fi can connect to the proxy. Only devices that know the username and password can use it. SOCKS5 sends that password without encryption, so use Wi-Fi connections only on networks you trust; the USB cable is safer. The proxy never accepts connections over the cellular network.

Children

Pocket Proxy does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@constructhub.us.
